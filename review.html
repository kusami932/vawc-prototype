Review scope and setup

Suggested ICA8 focus: BT1 C–D. Start at the homepage, search for two jobs, compare them, choose one, and identify its application next step. The intended user is a job seeker in user class JS1.

The full BT1: A — explore an administrative occupation; B — investigate a relevant training organization; C — compare two openings; D — choose an opening and identify how to apply. A C–D review alone does not establish completion of all four outcomes.

  • Start signed out with no prior selections. Use the reset button between evaluators.
  • Record evaluator ID, review date, prototype version, device/browser, and inspected scope.
  • Jobs and training records are demonstration data. Do not create an account, enroll, or submit an application.
  • Peripheral routes may show “not implemented.” Distinguish these documented limits from defects in the core task.

The supplied ICA8 preparation slide asks for at least five Nielsen heuristics, two original heuristics, and an interactive prototype. It does not specify evaluator count, severity grading, recording submission, or submission format. Follow any additional instructions given in class.

Task prompt: job search and comparison

You are looking for full-time administrative work within 25 miles of Richmond, Virginia. You prefer daytime work and pay of at least $19 per hour, or comparable annual pay. Find two relevant openings and compare their location, pay, schedule, and qualifications. Choose the opening you would investigate first, explain your choice and anything you still need to confirm, and find the next step toward applying. Stop before signing in, creating an account, or submitting anything.

Provide the prompt without demonstrating the ideal route or naming buttons. Missing pay or schedule information must remain unconfirmed; it should not be treated as evidence that the job meets the preference.

Extend the review to all of BT1

Also ask the evaluator to identify an administrative occupation and explain relevant duties and skills, then find a potentially relevant training organization and distinguish evidence of relevance from unconfirmed course availability. Record the reviewed scope as BT1 A–D.

Seven Nielsen heuristics

Use these questions to inspect the design. F01–F06 refer to the revised P3 usability issues.

H1 · Visibility of system status

After editing location or filters, is it clear whether changes are pending or applied? Do results show the active area and radius? Is the number of selected jobs visible?

P3: F03, F05

H2 · Match between system and the real world

Do labels distinguish an occupation from a vacancy and a provider from a program? Are search scope, pay units, and schedule labels understandable to a job seeker?

P3: F02, F04, F05

H3 · User control and freedom

Can users edit criteria, clear filters, deselect comparisons, return from details, and leave the application step without losing necessary context?

P3: F03, F05, F06

H4 · Consistency and standards

Do shared navigation, labels, buttons, selected tabs, and pending/applied states work consistently across occupation, training, and job views?

P3: F02, F03, F04

H5 · Error prevention

Are optional criteria distinguishable? Are invalid values explained before results update? Is comparison selection clear? Is the application destination identified before proceeding?

P3: F01, F03, F05, F06

H6 · Recognition rather than recall

Are labels and examples visible while typing? Can users compare employer, location, pay, schedule, and qualifications on one screen without remembering another browser tab?

P3: F02, F05

H8 · Aesthetic and minimalist design

Are common criteria prominent and specialized options grouped in expandable sections? Is the primary action easy to find without hiding necessary information?

P3: F01, F06

Two project-specific heuristics

D1 · Evidence-aware career decisions

Are full pay ranges preserved rather than implying a guaranteed maximum? Are missing pay and schedule shown as unknown? Is a training provider’s general relevance distinguished from current course enrollment availability?

P3: F04, F05

D2 · Distinct objects and useful next steps

Can users tell whether they are viewing an occupation, opening, organization, or program? Do related links preserve useful context? Does the application action identify the actual next step rather than a generic employer homepage?

P3: F04, F06

Record observable findings

  1. Inspect independently before discussing findings. First review the overall task flow, then examine details, edits, alternative routes, empty results, and error recovery.
  2. Record one distinct issue per template row: screen, action, observed behavior, heuristic, effect, proposed fix, and status. Add a screenshot reference where helpful.
  3. Consolidate duplicates after independent reviews, retaining evaluator IDs and evidence. Record changes and verify them in the revised prototype.

Download the blank CSV observation template. It contains 12 blank rows and can be extended. No evaluation findings are prefilled.

Optional severity and status conventions

This is an optional team convention, not a severity scheme specified in the supplied ICA8 slide: 0 = no issue after review; 1 = cosmetic; 2 = minor difficulty with an easy workaround; 3 = major difficulty or substantial extra effort; 4 = prevents an essential task outcome. Explain each rating from the observed effect.

Suggested status values: New, Needs clarification, Accepted, Deferred, Fixed, Verified, or Not an issue. Use Verified only after checking the change.

Heuristic evaluation produces expert assessments. These observations are not new participant completion rates, error counts, or satisfaction scores for the P2 baseline.

Carry the review into P4

Preserve the evaluated version. For each accepted finding, keep the original evidence, the change, its rationale, and before/after screenshots. Document tools, instructor/TA and peer feedback, AI use and supplied P3 artifacts, and limitations that could affect comparison with baseline measurements.

P4 requires a happy-path recording for each benchmark task, linked to the UX Target table. A full BT1 recording should cover A–D; a C–D recording should be labeled as a partial route. The supplied ICA8 preparation slide does not itself require these recordings.
